Order runs clockwise from the lowest move to the highest. Gainers pull together around 12 o’clock and losers are pushed away. Two tickers only trade places once one clearly leads, so near-ties don’t flicker. Dotted rings mark ±2.5% and ±5%.

% change shows each holding’s own move. $ impact shows what it did to your account, so a small move in a big position outweighs a big move in a small one.

Thickness is trading volume: today’s shares against a normal day at the same time (20-day average, weighted for the busier open and close).

Live follows prices through the day; drag the line under the ring to rewind today, and the center compares your day with the S&P 500 (SPY). Pills pulse when a holding flips between up and down or its volume passes 2×. Run brokerage_server.py for your holdings and real quotes; without it the page plays a simulated day.

Daily replay steps through closing values. Load CSV with date,ticker,value or date,ticker,shares,price (optional volume; a CASH row is cash; $SPY rows are the benchmark). The file stays in your browser.
